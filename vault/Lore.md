# Lore

The game's story so far. Keep new lines consistent with it. Back to [[Home]].

- **The city** is **Port Harrow**, on the coast ([[City Map]]).
- **The infection** is **the Bloom**. It came up out of the storm drains and the ground under the city square, then spread to the people.
- **Grave Titans** are what the Bloom grows into given time, made from the people it took ([[Waves and Boss]]).
- **Overwatch** is **Sgt. Voss**. He flies the supply drops and the deploy helicopters from a carrier offshore, and speaks every radio line ([[Radio config]], [[Match Start]]).
- **The safehouse** is a fortified ruin south of the city ([[Lobby]]). Its people ([[Survivors config]]):
  - **Rook**, old-timer, at the campfire
  - **Doc Hale**, medic, by the mess tent
  - **Cpl. Reyes**, radio operator, at the radio shack
  - **Sparks**, mechanic, at the Flight Line gate
  - **Big Mo**, bruiser, at the hoop
- **Where lore lives in code:** NPC lines and the radio-shack broadcasts in [[Survivors config]], radio lines in [[Radio config]], and tutorial lines in [[Tutorial config]].
