# UI Ideas

Menu, shop and reveal ideas from the owner's saved clips. Clips are listed in [[Video References]]. The screens in `UI REFERENCES` go with these. See also [[Store]] and [[Lobby]].

## Reveals

"UI References 1" is the one to study. Opening a gift in its shop goes:
1. The shop dims behind the gift.
2. The gift glows and pulses in the centre, changing colour between red and green.
3. It opens into the reward on a burst of gold rays, with a name plate saying what you unlocked.
4. The shop comes back.

**For us:** the same beats suit a Gem pack, a class unlock or a supply crate reward. Dim, build up, burst, name it. [[Levels]] already has a LEVEL UP! moment to match.

## Shop layout

From "Ui References", a colourful simulator's shop:
- Fat, chunky cards with one big icon each, in strong colours per category.
- A big bright price button on every card.
- A coloured ribbon like BEST or ONCE on the deals.
- An index screen with a clear count such as 8 / 54, where locked items show as black shapes.
- Rewards laid out as a grid of timed gifts, with a large special reward on day 7.

**For us:** the black-shape locked entries suit FIELD RESEARCH, which already locks each zombie until you kill one. The ribbon suits Store deals. Our look is darker than this, so take the layout, not the colours.

## Diegetic UI

"Unique UI ideas" has a character hold up a real 3D book as the stats screen, with pages that turn and idle movement. The menu exists in the world.

**For us:** a survivor's field notebook could be FIELD RESEARCH, or the Overwatch radio could be a held handset. It suits the safehouse setting. More work than a flat panel, so only for one showpiece screen.

## Weapon wheel

"Cool Ui animations" builds a radial wheel in Studio, in an ink-brush style with one slice lit red and a big name in the centre. Hovering moves the selection.

**For us:** a wheel suits a gamepad or phone, but we carry one gun at a time. It would fit a later emote or quick-chat menu better.

## Menus that stay quiet

"UI and loading screen ideas" and "Visual and UI Loadscreen References" use a thin text menu down one side, over one calm scene with nothing else competing: grass and a planted sword. A place name fades in when you enter an area.

**For us:** the place name idea fits entering the Commons or the Flight Line. Our lobby menu is busier by design, so this is a contrast to keep in mind, not a change to make.
